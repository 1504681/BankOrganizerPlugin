# In-game checks owed

Untested in the real client since 1.2.0 (hub builds auto-merge; the dashboard shows this until marked checked):

- Merge Categories → Combat: Gear. Open the bank: combat items in the Gear tab are not flagged misplaced, and Gear items in the Combat tab are.
- Ordering with a merge active: run the step-by-step reorder on the Gear tab. The phase label reads "Combat: sorting" for merged items and they end up grouped after the tab's own items.
- Quest vs Misc: a quest item (e.g. Ghostspeak amulet) lands in Quest; a random untradeable stackable lands in Misc.
- 1.2.2 categorisation: fruit and veg are Food; Confliction gauntlets and Avernic treads are Combat.
